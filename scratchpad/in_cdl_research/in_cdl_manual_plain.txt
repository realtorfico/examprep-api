Most operators of Commercial Motor Vehicles (CMV) must have a Commercial Driver's License (CDL). Some individuals are not required to hold a CDL to operate a CMV. This exception applies to certain military personnel, farmers, firefighters, emergency response vehicle operators and individuals removing snow and ice. For exception details and requirements review 49 CFR 383.3.
If you perform trade, traffic, or transport exclusively within your business' home state, this is considered intrastate commerce. If your trade, traffic, or transport is between a place in a state and a place outside of such state (including a place outside of the United States); between two places in a state through another state or a place outside of the United States; or between two places in a state as part of trade, traffic or transportation originating or terminating outside the state or the United States, this is considered interstate commerce.

CDL Knowledge Tests
If an applicant fails a CDL knowledge test, the applicant may take the test again the next business day. An applicant can only attempt a CDL knowledge test once a day. Once the CDL knowledge test has been passed the CDL test results are valid for one year.
How to Transfer an Out-of-Country or Out-of-State CDL
To transfer an out-of-state CDL to an Indiana CDL the applicant must meet the following requirements: · Pay all required fees. · Be at least 18 years of age. (To operate interstate commerce and/or apply for a school bus, applicant must be at least 21 years of age.) · Present documents of identification proving the applicant's identity, Social Security number, lawful status, and Indiana residency. A list of acceptable documents may be obtained on IN.gov/BMV or at any BMV branch. · Be a U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident. Lawful permanent residents must provide a valid, unexpired Permanent Resident Card, issued by USCIS or INS. (See Non-Domiciled CLP/CDL information if you are not a U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident.) · Have proof of an unexpired CDL and valid driving privileges. · Pass the general knowledge test. · Pass the hazardous materials knowledge test, if applicable. · Medical Examiner's Certificate on file with the BMV. · Provide the names of all states where previously licensed to operate any type of motor vehicle during the previous ten years. · Surrender the CDL from the previous state of residency to the BMV.
Out-of-state CDL refers to a CDL from any of the 50 U.S states including the District of Columbia, Canada and Mexico, but NOT United States territories Guam, Puerto Rico, American Samoa and the Virgin Islands. Any CDL that is not considered Out-of-State must retest for the CDL and apply for issuance of a CLP.
The CDL is an endorsement to an individual's driver's license. An applicant may be required to meet additional requirements for their license type.
Individuals have 30 days to transfer an out-of-state CDL.
The driver's license knowledge exam is only required if the out-of-state CDL is expired.

Exemption of Certain Medical Conditions
A person who is not physically qualified to operate a commercial motor vehicle under FMCSR 391.41 (b)(1) or (2) and who is otherwise qualified to drive a commercial motor vehicle, may operate a commercial motor vehicle if FMCSA has granted a medical exemption to that person.
For further information related to medical and health-related questions you may contact FMCSA at 800-832-5660.

Retesting Requirement
Any applicant with a CDL that has been expired, surrendered, disqualified, canceled, revoked, suspended or invalidated more than three years must pass all applicable knowledge and CDL skills test(s) as a first-time applicant.
CDL Skills Testing
An applicant must hold the CLP 14 days prior to attempting to CDL skills test and appointments must be scheduled at least two days in advance. The CDL skills test may only be taken once a business day. An applicant will be responsible for all costs each time that applicant has to take the CDL skills test. Once the CDL skills test has been passed, the applicant must visit any BMV branch for issuance of the appropriate CDL.
The CDL skills test results are valid for one year.
If an applicant fails any part of the skills test, fails to follow instructions, or performs an unsafe action, this will result in an automatic failure ending the CDL skills test. They will be required to reschedule the CDL skills test. Applicants are responsible for the CDL skills test fee and the truck rental fee, if applicable.
It shall be within the discretion of the CDL skills test examiner to determine if an applicant has operated the commercial motor vehicle in a manner that was dangerous or unsafe causing an automatic failure.
Once the applicant has passed the CDL skills test they must return to the BMV branch to complete the application process for the CDL prior to expiration of the CLP.
For additional information on the CDL skills test please review the following: · Section 1.1.2 Skills Tests · Section 11 Vehicle Inspection Test · Section 12 Basic Vehicle Control Skills Test · Section 13 On-road Driving
