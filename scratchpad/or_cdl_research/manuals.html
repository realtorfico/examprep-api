The Oregon Driver Manual is online in English and Spanish, and it is also accessible to web translation tools. It has information about road signs, traffic laws and other information you need to know to drive in Oregon, and to pass the standard Knowledge Test (for Class C non-commercial).  Use the languages function (upper right-hand corner of the webpage) to translate the manual to whichever language you prefer.​ ​

 

While you may take the Oregon Knowledge Test as many times as you need, it is highly recommended that you study and review the Oregon Driver Manual to ensure you pass the Knowledge Test the first time! For more information on our Pass/Fail rates, please visit our Statistics page​.
 
Note: The manual is also available in audio format in English and Spanish, and in PDF form, also in English ​or Spanish​. In order to view important illustrations and diagrams, we recommend you use the online manual along with the audio version.

​          

The Oregon Commercial Driver Manual​ is now accessible to web translation tools.  It has the information you need to drive a commercial vehicle in Oregon and to pass various Commercial Knowledge and Skills Tests. The manual is available in audio format and in PDF format.
 
Note: In order to view important illustrations and diagrams, we recommend you use a hardcopy manual​ along with the audio version.

​          

The Oregon Motorcycle & Moped Manual is now accessible to web translation tools. The information covers motorcycle and moped road rules and safe riding practices. It has the information you need to pass the Motorcycle Knowledge Test, if required. Use the translate function (upper right-hand corner) to translate the manual to whichever language you prefer.​​

Note: The manual is also available in PDF format or audio format. In order to view important illustrations and diagrams, we recommend you use the online manual along with the audio version.​

​  

The How to Guide Your Teen Driver is now accessible to web translation tools. The information covers information to help you prepare your teen for driving. It has the information you need to pass the Oregon driver knowledge test. Use the translate function (upper right-hand corner) to translate the manual to whichever language you prefer.​​

Note: The manual is also available PDF format: English and Espa​ñol​.

​
The Oregon Vehicle License Plate Manual has information about the different Oregon license plates. Contact DMV if you need information about plates not listed in the manual.

​

The Guide to Oregon Driving Records & Standard Conviction Abbreviation Manual has complete instructions for reading Oregon driving records.

​

The Vehicle Title and Registration Handbook is designed to help Oregon certified dealers and other businesses prepare applications to title and register vehicles in Oregon.
